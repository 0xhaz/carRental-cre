## Revenue Distribution System

## Contract

- [RevenueDistributor.sol](./RevenueDistributor.sol) - Revenue waterfall implementation:
  - 15% Platform Fee → Protocol treasury
  - 10% Maintenance Reserve → Per-vehicle escrow
  - 5% Insurance → Coverage payments
  - 10% Operating Costs → Gas, cleaning, etc.
  - 60% Net Distributable → RevenueToken holders
  - Batch distribution support
  - Complete accounting and history tracking
